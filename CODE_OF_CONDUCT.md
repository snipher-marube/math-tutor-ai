# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text applies, and the summary below is for convenience.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to first-time contributors.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the learners and the community.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a project built for children

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers through the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or by contacting a maintainer directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code.
