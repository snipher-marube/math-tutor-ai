# Curriculum scope: CBE Grade 2 Mathematics

Kenya's Competency Based Education (CBE, formerly CBC) organises Lower Primary Mathematics Activities into **strands** and **sub-strands**. This file defines what the tutor should cover.

> ⚠️ **Verify before relying on this.** This is a working summary written for contributors. It is not an official document. The source of truth is the **KICD Grade 2 Mathematics Activities Curriculum Design**. If you have the official document, please help us check this page, add learning-outcome references, and fix any gaps (use the *Curriculum / learning content* issue template).

## Strands and sub-strands

### 1. Numbers
| Sub-strand | What learners do (summary) |
| --- | --- |
| Number concept | Sort, group, match and order objects, and make number patterns |
| Whole numbers | Count, read and write numbers (typically up to 100), work with place value (tens and ones), compare numbers, and use number words |
| Addition | Add numbers within 100, with and without regrouping. Includes word problems |
| Subtraction | Subtract within 100, with and without regrouping, and relate subtraction to addition |
| Multiplication | Multiplication as repeated addition, with small numbers |
| Division | Division as sharing and grouping equally, with small numbers |
| Fractions | Recognise ½ and ¼ of a whole and of a group |

### 2. Measurement
| Sub-strand | What learners do (summary) |
| --- | --- |
| Length | Compare lengths and measure with non-standard units, then the metre |
| Mass | Compare mass, from non-standard units up to the kilogram |
| Capacity | Compare capacity, from non-standard units up to the litre |
| Time | Days of the week, months of the year, and telling time by the hour and half hour |
| Money | Recognise Kenyan coins and notes, and add, subtract and give change in small amounts (KSh) |

### 3. Geometry
| Sub-strand | What learners do (summary) |
| --- | --- |
| Lines | Straight and curved lines, and drawing lines |
| Shapes | Recognise and sort rectangles, squares, triangles and circles, and make patterns with shapes |

## How the tutor should teach

- **Concrete → pictorial → abstract.** Start with objects ("5 mangoes"), then pictures or tallies, then numbers.
- **Kenyan context.** Use shillings, sukuma wiki, matatus, school items, local names, and market scenes.
- **Short steps.** One idea per sentence, and three to five steps at most.
- **Language.** Simple English at a Grade 2 reading level. Kiswahili support is planned.
- **Encourage.** Praise effort and invite the learner to try the next step themselves.

## Out of scope

- Content above Grade 2 (long multiplication, decimals, negative numbers, and so on). The tutor should gently say it's a topic for later grades.
- Non-maths subjects. The tutor should steer back to maths.
